# Product decisions log

Decisions taken by the platform owner in response to the six-month simulation ([SIMULATION-REPORT.md](SIMULATION-REPORT.md)). Numbers refer to the list put to the owner on 2026-10-22; gap ids refer to the report's gap register.

| # | Gaps | Question | Decision | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | FIN-01, PF-04 | Who pays whom? | Hosts pay the platform; the platform pays suppliers. The platform carries the float and the credit risk. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 2 | PA-04 | Is the agency's own bank a supplier like any other? | No: own-bank supply is the agency's own sales (gross revenue, worker pay as cost of sales), not a supplier statement. No platform fee on bank shifts; margin reported separately. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 3 | PA-01 | Floor rates and uplifts | Platform-wide floor rates. The floor charge rate must cover at least the minimum wage payable to the worker plus the platform fee. Enforced (a shift cannot be posted below the floor). | Decided 2026-10-22. Default taken: the floor also covers employer on-costs at a configurable percentage (25%), so floor charge = minimum wage × (1 + on-cost %) ÷ (1 − platform fee %). The minimum-wage figure is a platform setting. |
| 4 | TRS-01 | Penalties for late cancellations and no-shows | A no-show is a disciplinary matter for the worker's employer. The platform records no-shows per employer; repeated no-shows trigger a penalty or suspension of the employer on the platform. | Decided 2026-10-22. Defaults taken: 2 no-shows in 90 days remove the employer's priority access for 30 days; 3 suspend it pending review. Thresholds are platform settings. |
| 5 | PF-03 | Clocked or scheduled time? | Hosts pay clocked time. | Decided 2026-10-22. Defaults taken: a host queries a statement line; the platform admin raises the credit; credits net off the host's next invoice and the supplier's next remittance. No rounding: clocked minutes are paid. |
| 6 | DSP-01 | Automatic escalation of unfilled shifts | Each host chooses automatic or manual escalation; automatic uses a host-set rate ceiling and widening steps. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 7 | PS-01 | How long an unanswered request holds a worker | A pending request is a soft hold: a firm (confirmed) booking elsewhere takes priority over it. If a host leaves a request unanswered for some time, the platform operator is reminded to get in touch with the host. | Decided 2026-10-22. Default taken: the operator is reminded 12 hours after an unanswered request, or 24 hours before the shift starts, whichever is sooner (platform settings). |
| 8 | WF-03 | Assume full contracted hours unless told otherwise | Yes. | Decided 2026-10-22. Default taken: the employer can record the hours a worker actually has with them for any ISO week (`PUT /workers/:id/weeks/:monday`, or in bulk), and that figure replaces the contracted hours in the 48-hour check for that week alone. A surplus flag can name the staff it frees, which records them as having no rostered hours (or a stated figure) with their employer for every week of the flag; withdrawing the flag puts them back on their contracted hours from the current week on. |
| 9 | WF-01 | Do verified documents and ratings follow a worker to a new employer (with consent)? | Yes. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 10 | ONB-03, PH-01, PH-03 | One organisation with site-scoped managers, or parent and child organisations? | One organisation with its sites (e.g. a group with 8 homes). Managers can be scoped to one or more sites; workers have a home site; the group admin sees everything. Moving staff between a group's own homes stays outside Merge. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 11 | PR-02 | Compliance evidence that may cross organisations | Each employer is responsible for ensuring its workers meet the standard mandatory requirements for care workers; hosts see a "meets mandatory requirements" status, not document details. Anything beyond the standard (specific training such as PEG feeding) is stated on the platform by the host, and suppliers declare which workers hold it. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 12 | PT-01 | Checks before a new organisation joins | Must be a legitimate care company (CQC registration or equivalent, checked at approval) and must agree to the memorandum of understanding (MOU) before approval. Registration creates a PENDING organisation; the platform admin approves. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 13 | SEC-01, PT-02 | Controller/processor stance; operator access to tenant data | The operator holds worker data to run the platform but does not need to see worker pay rates or DBS details; those stay with the employer (consistent with 11). | Decided 2026-10-22 for access. Working assumption until a lawyer confirms: the operator is a processor for employers' worker data and a controller for platform account data. |
| 14 | PC-01, PM-01, PM-02 | Safeguarding concerns about a lent worker | The worker is accountable to their employer. Under the MOU every employer must have a framework for handling reports about a lent worker from the host. The platform records a report and passes it to the employer; it does not mediate. Hosts can bar individual workers from their shifts. | Decided 2026-10-22 |
| 15 | COM-01, ONB-02 | Email and SMS providers; who pays for SMS | To be decided at deployment. Build against a provider-neutral channel interface. | Deferred |
| 16 | PT-03 | Two-factor authentication and single sign-on | Two-factor authentication is compulsory for admins and managers. | Decided 2026-10-22. Default taken: authenticator-app (TOTP) two-factor built in and compulsory for admins and managers; Microsoft single sign-on as the first external provider, after go-live. |
| 17 | PT-06 | Which rota systems to integrate first | Depends on what clients use; to be done after go-live. | Deferred |
| 18 | CMP-03 | Document storage and retention | Default taken: files stored through a storage adapter (local disk in development, S3-compatible bucket in a UK region in production); kept 6 years after the worker leaves, then deleted. | Default 2026-10-22 |
